# Release audit and interpretation

Audit date: 2026-10-01. The release preserves observed results, including failures. It changes interpretation where implementation or chronology requires it.

1. **Calendar boundary.** Legacy Core-92 development uses characteristic months January 2010–December 2019, hence return months February 2010–January 2020. Core-86 uses return months January 2010–December 2019. Thus the later 2020–2025 evaluation is locked at the model/protocol stage but is not a wholly unseen holdout for the research process. Historical `sealed` file names and local `preregistered` labels are preserved as archive identifiers, not independent registration claims.
2. **Feature lineage.** Core-86 uses public benchmark values where nonmissing and self-built values otherwise. It is a bridge, not a wholly independent reconstruction. Comparing Core-92 and Core-86 changes coverage, construction and timing as well as six features. The 172 network inputs are 86 economic characteristics and 86 missing indicators.
3. **CRSP source.** Monthly returns were compounded from CIZ daily total returns, including their embedded delisting treatment. The input was not a CRSP Monthly Stock File download. Accounting records use availability-lag conventions on a downloaded vintage, not a point-in-time unrevised accounting archive.
4. **Model scope.** The empirical pricing networks are a fixed diagnostic family selected after an earlier benchmark advancement failure. They are not established here as state-of-the-art models or representative of every ML asset-pricing method.
5. **Theory.** The proposition covers a finite set of nested, known linear spans with independent Gaussian means and a fixed positive weight matrix. It is not a general theorem about deep networks. The asymptotic penalty result is illustrative; the release does not claim empirically calibrated dimension confidence sets or verified performance of that penalty on stocks.
6. **Simulation.** Both evaluation modes know the loading matrix and covariance. Feasible means a noisy evaluation mean. Oracle evaluation prevents over-selection by construction in this nested setup. Grid-level intervals summarize heterogeneous specified conditions, not an independent population sample of economies.
7. **Temporal exercise.** The 214-input return predictors are separate models. Future-validation eligibility, complete state histories, historical preprocessing and previously chosen neural hyperparameters condition the sample/design. Monthly lagged update decisions do not make the full exercise a prospective investable backtest. Neural/ridge refits are monthly; tree refits are annual. The CUSUM rule is an internal historical exercise after broader history had been examined.
8. **Representation measurements.** Spectral diagnostics cover 120 months; nearest-neighbor and tangent-angle diagnostics use ten sampled months. The tangent statistic measures scale-dependent subspace angles and is not a differential-geometric curvature estimator. Numerical near-rank-one collapse is distinct from supervised classification neural collapse. The geometry probe predicts separately measured model-level pricing endpoints, not individual stock returns or a causal pricing effect.
9. **Public reproducibility.** The release verifies existing aggregate evidence and rebuilds exhibits. It excludes licensed security observations, stock-level panels and predictions, checkpoint weights and stock-level factor-construction records. Version 1.1.0 adds the 60 models’ aggregate monthly managed-factor returns for statistical replay; these do not disclose individual holdings or observations. Retraining requires lawfully obtained inputs and appropriate runtime/hardware. Changed vendor vintages and floating-point implementations can change results; exact reproduction is not guaranteed across these changes.
10. **Later bounded controls.** The random-network and same-panel masking controls were frozen before their own execution but after the initial manuscript/archive existed. They use familiar 2010–2019 data and are exploratory. Absolute rank decline partly reflects decreasing layer width; trained networks show additional concentration relative to matched random networks. Same-panel masking does not reproduce the archived negative rank correlations. Twelve pointwise paired intervals are conditional on trained models and validation coefficients, not simultaneous tests or structural-dimension confidence sets. Failed preparation/training attempts led only to data-reader and Torch linear-solve compatibility repairs, not changed scientific specifications. All 60 completed models are reported.
11. **Publication status.** This is a replication archive for an author manuscript, not proof of journal acceptance or a claim to be the first study of low-dimensional asset-pricing representations.

The 60 research references were checked against DOI registries or official proceedings pages. Bibliographic audit records identify their sources. The additional archive citation refers to this release. Initial journal submission uses the official Elsevier `elsarticle` class without publisher logos or fictitious volume, DOI, acceptance or copyright information. Author and repository identifiers are separated from the anonymous submission file.
