\section{Boundary of the economic-use evidence}

A separate return-prediction replay examines updating rather than the dimension of the pricing networks. It covers 239 months and 1,043,511 stock-months. Its universe conditions on future-validation eligibility and complete state history, and its preprocessing and model choices use overlapping research history. We therefore treat it as a retrospective selected-sample diagnostic. Respecting the lag between an observed signal and a portfolio decision does not make the entire experiment prospective.

Within that sample, an exploratory common change in updating value occurs after April 2014, whereas the fixed sequential rule first triggers in August 2018. None of the six primary update-minus-frozen portfolio comparisons passes the Holm-adjusted 5\% criterion. These results neither identify changes in pricing dimension nor establish implementable gains in a prospectively selected stock universe. Supplement~D retains the decomposition, complete results, cost comparisons, and temporal figure; they are supporting evidence rather than a main identification result.
