\input{author_frontmatter}

\begin{abstract}
Selecting a compact asset-pricing network does not by itself identify the number of priced risks. We examine this distinction using U.S. equity networks, matched controls, and known-truth simulations. In an archived diagnostic family, preferred dimensions vary across payoff spans, loss weights, data pipelines, and periods. A later same-panel feature ablation does not reproduce the archived pipeline rank reversal, limiting its attribution to feature removal. A conditional-autoencoder extension gives a different pattern: eight factors, the candidate-grid upper bound, attain the lowest development pricing distance in 87.2\% of resampling draws. Thus selection instability is not common to every tested family. Its predictive $R^2$ is 0.269\%, and its HJ-type distance is below FF5 plus momentum in point estimates, without an established superiority claim. A separate calibration finds only 83.5\% and 87.0\% simultaneous coverage for nominal 95\% dimension-comparison intervals in the two primary equal-distance, 120-month scenarios. These intervals cannot establish equivalence or structural nonidentification. In the original geometry analysis, training adds concentration beyond matched random networks, but the specified incremental pricing-loss prediction criterion is not met. The post-study controls use familiar historical data. The evidence distinguishes representation compactness, candidate selection, and economic dimension, while delimiting the conclusions supported by each experiment.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
The replication package provides construction and analysis code, frozen configurations, synthetic results, aggregate figure and table data, and checksums. The section-ordered package includes the conditional-autoencoder control (P1-G4-V003) and its inference calibration (P1-G4-V004), including unsuccessful calibration results. CRSP, Compustat, CCM, third-party security-level characteristic records, model checkpoints, stock-level predictions, and derived security-level panels are excluded. Reconstruction requires independently licensed inputs. Public source downloads are linked rather than relicensed. Aggregate exhibit replay is separate from retraining with licensed data, and no cross-platform bitwise retraining guarantee is made.

Version 1.2.1 is archived at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23177771}{10.5281/zenodo.23177771}, with code at \href{https://github.com/extradimen/asset-pricing-dimension-replication}{GitHub}. Earlier releases remain available under their original identifiers.
